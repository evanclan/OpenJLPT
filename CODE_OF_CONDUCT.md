# Code of conduct

OpenJLPT is a community of learners, teachers, native speakers and developers. We follow the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/):
be welcoming and patient (many contributors are still learning Japanese or English), assume good
faith, give and accept feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the project and its
users. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated.

If something makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer privately through
[GitHub](https://github.com/evanclan) or open an issue if it's appropriate to discuss publicly.
Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits and issues that break these rules, and
may block people who repeatedly do so.
